Win顺 0.33.1（自用构建）合并上游 0.33.0：文件夹菜单改成多列并排展开，父列保留、逐层向右；另存为对话框旁的搜索框不再乱跳，保持在对话框下方，放不下时停在屏幕底部（像 Listary）；新增图钉、`winshun://` 设置链接、“密集”界面大小、Tab 进入文件夹、插件设置滑块和内容详情面板。自用的「运行」托盘图标、开关与范围记忆继续保留。 WinShun 0.33.1 (a personal build) merges upstream 0.33.0: the folder menu opens its columns side by side, parents staying; the bar by Save As no longer jumps, staying under the dialog and at the screen's foot when it must, as Listary does; pins, `winshun://` links, the Dense size, Tab into a folder, sliders in plugin settings and the content details panel are new. The personal 运行 tray icons, switch and scope memory stay.

## 新功能 · What's new

- ⬆️ **同步上游 0.33.0** 文件夹菜单多列并排，父列保留、逐层向右，指针停在行上一会儿就展开下一列；另存为旁的搜索框保持在对话框下方，放不下时停在屏幕底部、列表向上展开（#362）；图钉可以原位编辑并显示按键卡片；`winshun://settings/<section>` 直接打开设置页；“密集”界面大小一行一条；Tab 进入选中文件夹、`Ctrl+Backspace` 上一级；插件设置的数字范围改为滑块；内容详情面板可读匹配内容；截图支持 Tab 选窗口、Shift+方向键微调。详见上游 0.33.0 的发布说明。

  **Upstream 0.33.0 merged** The folder menu opens columns side by side, parents staying and the next column coming as the pointer rests on a row; the bar by Save As stays under the dialog, and at the screen's foot with its list opening upward when it must (#362); pins can be edited in place and show a card of their keys; `winshun://settings/<section>` opens that settings page; the Dense size gives one line per result; Tab goes into the chosen folder and `Ctrl+Backspace` steps out; plugin number settings get sliders; the content details panel reads the matches; capture picks whole windows with Tab and nudges with Shift+arrows. See the upstream 0.33.0 notes.

- 🧩 **自用保留** 运行页的托盘图标、全部搜索里的托盘程序与「运行」标签、托盘开关（默认开）、范围标签记忆、双击记忆，均继续保留。

  **Kept from the self-build** The tray rows in 运行, the tray programs and 运行 tags in 全部, the tray switch (on by default), the scope tab remembered across openings, and the double-click memory stay.

- 🔌 **插件** 截图与录屏 1.1.19、图片文字搜索 1.0.18（从插件库更新）。

  **Plugins** Screenshot and recording 1.1.19 and image text search 1.0.18, from the plugin library.
