## 新功能 · What's new

- 🖱️ **悬停行显示选中样式** 鼠标悬停的结果行现在穿上键盘选中行的样式：白底、圆角边框、左侧竖条（brand 主题），原选中行的样式同时取消。主搜索窗和对话框旁的搜索条都这样；列表的当前行高亮在悬停别行时让位。

  **The hovered row wears the current row's face** The row under the pointer shows the raised face, the rounded border and the left bar (brand) that the keyboard's row had, and that row loses them. The launcher and the dialog bar alike; the list's own highlight steps aside meanwhile.

- ⌨️ **方向键与鼠标互斥** 鼠标最后移动过时，按 `↑/↓` 从鼠标悬停行继续移动（一次一格），按键后键盘接管，鼠标停着不动不会抢回；鼠标再次移动立即夺回样式。滚动或行复用时产生的合成悬停事件不再被当成鼠标移动。

  **The keys and the pointer take turns** When the pointer moved last, ↑/↓ move on from the row under it, one row a press; after a key the keyboard's row wears the face wherever the pointer rests, and the pointer moving again takes it back. Hovers a scroll or a reused row brings are no moves.

- 🐞 **修复：白底遮住行内容** 列表的当前行高亮原来绘制在行内容的上层，品牌主题的不透明白底会盖住整行文字；现在压到内容下层，文字与图标始终可见。另：名字为空时不再弹出空的重命名框；brand 主题的左侧竖条在当前行也显示（原来只在多选时显示）。

  **Fixed: the white face hiding a row** The list's current-row highlight drew above the rows' words, and brand's opaque white face could hide a whole row; it draws under them now. Also: no empty rename box when there is no name to type over; brand's left bar shows on the current row too, not only on a multi-selection.

- 🧩 **自用保留** 运行页的托盘图标、全部搜索里的托盘程序与「运行」标签、托盘开关（默认开）、范围标签记忆、双击记忆，均继续保留。

  **Kept from the self-build** The tray rows in 运行, the tray programs and 运行 tags in 全部, the tray switch (on by default), the scope tab remembered across openings, and the double-click memory stay.
