## 新功能 · What's new

- ⬅️ **设置窗口前进/后退** 标题栏上的箭头、`Alt+←/→`、鼠标侧键都能后退、前进，回去还在原来的标签和位置；页面滚到底再往下滚就到下一页；相关设置之间可以跳转，“系统整合”里的文件夹菜单单独成一页；托盘图标的左键、中键行为可以自己选，还能设一个快捷键直接打开设置。

  **Back and forward in Settings** The title bar's arrows, Alt+←/→ and the mouse's side buttons go back and forward, each page as it was left; a wheel turn past the bottom goes to the next page; related settings link to each other, and the folder menu has a page of its own under Integration; the tray icon's left and middle clicks are chosen, and a hotkey opens Settings.

- 🔎 **搜索与结果** 搜不到东西时，会提示被排除的目录、已跳过的文件里有没有它，点一下这次也搜；结果可以以管理员身份在终端中打开（#425）；列表滚动后 1–9 编号跟着看得到的行走，滚动条与数字提示更明确。

  **Search and results** A search that finds nothing tells whether the excluded folders or skipped files have it, and one click searches them this time; a result opens in a terminal as administrator (#425); the 1–9 hints follow the rows in sight after scrolling, with clearer scroll ranges and number tips.

- 📋 **剪贴板与截图** Win顺 自己的截图是否记进剪贴板历史可以单独设（全部 / 仅复制的 / 关闭）；剪贴板的预览和看大图窗口有了边框和阴影；钉住的搜索框在任务栏上显示 Win顺 的图标，原生句柄重建后边框与任务栏状态也保持。

  **Clipboard and screenshots** Whether WinShun's own screenshots join the clipboard history is a choice (all / copied only / off); the clipboard's preview and the picture viewer get borders and shadows; the pinned box shows WinShun's icon on the taskbar and keeps its frame and taskbar state across native handle recreation.

- 🧩 **自用保留** 运行页的托盘图标、全部搜索里的托盘程序与「运行」标签、托盘开关（默认开）、范围标签记忆、双击记忆，均继续保留。

  **Kept from the self-build** The tray rows in 运行, the tray programs and 运行 tags in 全部, the tray switch (on by default), the scope tab remembered across openings, and the double-click memory stay.
