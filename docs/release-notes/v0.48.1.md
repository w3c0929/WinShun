## 新功能 · What's new

- 💾 **剪贴板备份与还原** 剪贴板历史可以备份成一个 ZIP，换电脑后还原；设置里会提示一次已还原。

  **Clipboard backup and restore** The clipboard history backs up into a ZIP and restores on another PC, said once in Settings.

- 🔢 **类型标签带数量** “文件”的类型标签上显示每种类型找到多少个；预览的类型标签校验按自身检查通过。

  **Type chips with counts** The Files type chips say how many matches each kind has.

- 🔤 **增强文字识别可换模型** 可以加装读日文的较大模型（PP-OCRv6 rec small），也能用自己下载的模型。

  **Enhanced OCR, a model of your choosing** An optional larger model that reads Japanese, or one you download yourself.

- 🥧 **收藏圆盘** 拖文件时在文件夹上停一下就展开它里面的文件夹（#463）。

  **Drop pie** Resting on a folder opens the folders inside it (#463).

- 🎨 **界面统一** 全部图标改为按容器排版（尺寸即容器），类型条与选项标签用控件的圆角而非胶囊形，名称与图标留出间距；插件“使用说明 / 更新内容”对话框重新排版，滚动条拖动后不再空白、不再抖动。

  **A consistent interface** Icons are laid out by their container everywhere, chips take a control's corner instead of a capsule, and names sit clear of their icons; the plugin "how to use / what's new" dialogs are laid out for reading, with no blank page or jitter after a scrollbar drag.

- 🧩 **自用保留** 运行页的托盘图标、全部搜索里的托盘程序与「运行」标签、托盘开关（默认开）、范围标签记忆、双击记忆，均继续保留。

  **Kept from the self-build** The tray rows in 运行, the tray programs and 运行 tags in 全部, the tray switch (on by default), the scope tab remembered across openings, and the double-click memory stay.
